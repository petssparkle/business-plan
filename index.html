02The Wedge — Why Customers Will Switch

Lodgify, Hostaway, and Guesty are crowded. We're not competing on parity — we're sharper in three specific ways.

Wedge 1

Beautiful Owner Websites

A property owner gets a fast, modern, multi-language (EN + AR) booking website in under 30 minutes. This was Lodgify's original wedge — we ship a 2026 version of it with better design and an integrated booking widget.

Wedge 2

AI Check-in & Check-out

WhatsApp-native pre-arrival flow, automated access codes, multilingual concierge, and post-stay review collection — all powered by Claude. Almost no incumbent has built this well.

Wedge 3

One Calendar, Four Channels

iCal 2-way sync to Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, and Expedia from day one. Zero double-bookings. The single feature that will retain every customer we win.

05Product Readiness — Go-Live Checklist

No public launch until every one of these is a confident yes. Belal owns this gate.

  • A property owner can sign up, list a property, and see a working booking website in under 30 minutes
  • An inquiry from Airbnb auto-creates a booking and blocks the calendar on all other channels within 5 minutes
  • Double-bookings are impossible — proven by stress-test of 50 concurrent simulated bookings
  • AI WhatsApp pre-arrival message fires automatically 48h before check-in, in EN + AR
  • Payment via Tap or Stripe Connect is tested end-to-end — including refund and partial refund flows
  • Sentry + PostHog + uptime alerts are wired up — every error and every signup visible in real-time
  • Public pricing page, sign-up flow, Calendly demo link, and 2-min Loom intro video are all live
  • At least 1 real customer has been using the product in production for 2+ weeks with zero P0 bugs
  • Privacy policy, terms of service, and refund policy are published
  • Daily Postgres backup is running and a restore has been successfully tested once

The 6 acquisition channels (priority order)

Channel 1 — Target: 4–6 customers

Personal & Walego network

Make a list of 50 property owners you know personally — through Walego, hospitality, friends-of-friends. Message every one individually with a 2-min Loom video. Highest trust, fastest close, ~10% conversion. Expected start: Week 1.

Conversion rate to paid: ~10%. Touchpoints needed: 50.

Channel 2 — Target: 5–7 customers

Cleaning & maintenance partnerships

Cleaning companies have 10–50 property-owner clients each and direct trust with them. Offer 20% revenue share for referrals. Sign 3–5 cleaning partnerships in Months 2–4. One of the highest-ROI channels in this entire industry.

Outreach plan: 30 cleaning companies → 5 sign partnership → 1–2 customers per partnership.

Channel 3 — Target: 4–6 customers

Cold outbound to Airbnb hosts

Scrape public Airbnb listings in target cities → find host details on Instagram or via their listing → DM/email a targeted pitch with the property name. Volume game: 200 outreaches → ~20 demos → ~5 customers.

Cadence: 25 outreaches per week starting Month 2.

Channel 4 — Target: 2–3 customers

Real-estate & tourism partnerships

Real-estate agencies selling vacation homes have post-sale relationships with buyers. Co-marketing: free white-label dashboard for the agency, intros for you. Same play with local concierge services and tourism boards.

Target: 5 agencies signed, 1 customer per agency in Year 1.

Channel 5 — Target: 1–2 customers

Industry WhatsApp / Telegram groups

Join 10 relevant property-management groups in EN + AR. Be useful for 30 days before you mention the product. Then mention it organically — never broadcast. Slow burn, but every customer here also brings 1–2 referrals.

Time investment: 30 min/day, every day.

Channel 6 — Target: 0 customers Year 1

SEO content (long-term play)

Start writing now even though it won't pay off in 2026. Target keywords: "vacation rental website builder", "Airbnb check-in automation", "channel manager Dubai". Write 2 posts per week starting Week 1 — pays off in 2027.

Goal: 50+ indexed posts by Dec 31.

Demo & sales process

  • Calendly link with 30-min slots — Mahmoud takes every demo for the first 20
  • Pre-call (cuts no-shows ~50%): send the 2-min Loom + 1-page PDF before every call
  • Call structure: 5 min discovery → 15 min live demo → 5 min pricing → 5 min commit-to-pilot ask
  • Send proposal within 24h with a 7-day acceptance window (urgency)
  • Follow-up cadence: +3 days (send a relevant tip), +7 days (case-study or comparison), +14 days (final ask)
  • Log every demo into a simple Notion or Attio CRM — never miss a follow-up

First-customer success playbook

  • White-glove onboarding for all 20: Mahmoud or Belal sets up each customer's listings personally on a 60-min Zoom
  • Day 7 check-in: "How is the calendar sync working? Any inquiries come through?"
  • Day 30 check-in: "What's the one thing missing that would make this a 10/10?"
  • Day 60 NPS survey: simple 1-question form, in their language
  • Day 90 case-study ask: 15-min recorded interview → public case study + a referral ask
  • Slack / WhatsApp group for all customers — turns them into a community, reduces support load, generates feature ideas

11Top Risks for Phase 1

Five things most likely to kill the plan. Plan for them now.

Risk 1 — Calendar sync bugs

Double-bookings will lose customers in days, not weeks. Mitigation: Belal builds a sync test suite from week 1. Stress-test with 50 concurrent bookings before any public launch.

Risk 2 — Slipping launch timeline

If V1 isn't ready by October, we miss the 20-customer goal. Mitigation: Hire contract dev in July. Cut scope aggressively (no smart locks, no mobile app, no analytics dashboard in V1).

Risk 3 — Wrong customer acquisition channels

If Channels 1–3 don't deliver the first 10 customers by October, we have 3 months left to find another channel. Mitigation: Weekly CAC-per-channel review. Kill any channel that hasn't produced a customer by Month 4.

Risk 4 — Founder bandwidth

Two people doing 5 jobs each for 7.5 months will burn out. Mitigation: One full day off per week, non-negotiable. Hire the contract dev specifically to relieve Belal's workload.

Risk 5 — Pricing too low

$29–79/mo for 1–5 properties may not cover CAC. Mitigation: Test annual prepay early. If first 10 customers all pick Growth ($79), consider raising prices to $99 for new customers in Q4.

13The Bottom Line

The next 7.5 months have one job: prove someone will pay us for this product. Twenty paying customers by December 31 is the only number that matters. Everything else — the perfect feature set, the slick brand, the long-term strategy — is noise until that's done.

Mahmoud is full-time on getting customers — interviews, outreach, demos, content, partnerships, support. Belal is full-time on shipping a V1 that the first 20 customers won't churn from. One contract developer joins in Month 3 to keep velocity up. No other hires. No office. No fundraising — yet.

If we end December 2026 with 20 happy paying customers and a stable V1, we have all the leverage we need to plan 2027 from a position of strength — investors, hires, geography, the whole thing. If we don't, we'll know exactly what to fix.

Now go build.
