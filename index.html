01The Product & Pricing

Demo process

  • Mahmoud runs every demo personally for the first 20
  • Pre-call: 2-min Loom + 1-pager (halves no-shows)
  • 30 min: 5 discovery → 15 demo → 5 pricing → 5 commit
  • Proposal sent within 24h, 7-day acceptance window
  • Follow up at +3d, +7d, +14d

First-customer success

  • White-glove onboarding: 60-min Zoom each, listings set up by us
  • Day 7 / 30 / 60 / 90 check-ins
  • Day 90: case-study interview + referral ask
  • Customer WhatsApp group — community, not support tickets

Top 3 risks

1 — Calendar sync bugs. Double-bookings lose customers in days. Belal builds sync test suite Week 1; no launch until 50 concurrent bookings pass.

2 — Slipping October launch. Belal solo on the build. Mitigation: cut scope aggressively before missing the date.

3 — Channels 1–3 don't deliver. If we don't hit 10 customers by end of October, year-end goal is gone. Weekly channel review; kill any channel without a customer by Month 4.

What comes after Phase 1 (Jan 2027): Close the investor, founders start drawing salaries, and hire 5 — 1 developer (relieves Belal), 1 marketer, and 3 sales reps (outbound cold calls + demos). Mahmoud moves from running every demo himself to managing the 3-person sales team. The 20 paying customers we close in Phase 1 are the proof that unlocks all of it.
