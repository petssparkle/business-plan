05What Could Kill This

Five risks, ranked. Each has one specific mitigation.

1 — Calendar sync bugs

Double-bookings will lose customers in days. Fix: Belal builds sync test suite Week 1. No public launch until 50 concurrent simulated bookings pass.

2 — Slipping the October launch

If V1 isn't ready by Oct, we miss 20 customers. Fix: Hire contract dev in July. Cut scope aggressively if any feature is at risk.

3 — Channels 1–3 don't deliver

If we don't have 10 customers by end of Oct, the year-end goal is gone. Fix: Weekly CAC-per-channel review. Kill any channel without a customer by Month 4.

4 — Founder burnout

Two people, 7.5 months, 5 hats each. Fix: One full day off per week, non-negotiable. Contract dev relieves Belal in Month 3.

5 — Pricing too low

$29–79 may not cover CAC. Fix: If first 10 customers all pick Growth, raise new-customer pricing to $99 in Q4.

The only number that matters: 20 paying customers on Dec 31, 2026. Mahmoud finds them. Belal builds something they won't churn from. One contract dev keeps the build on schedule. No office, no team, no fundraising — yet. Hit 20 by year-end and 2027 plans itself.
