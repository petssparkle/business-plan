Two ways customers convert: (1) booked demo → sales partner closes, or (2) 7-day free trial self-serve sign-up that converts to a paid plan. Both paths feed the same funnel. Onboarding ladder: first 5 clients (design partners) get 3 months free for weekly feedback; clients 6–15 get 50% off for 6 months (paid pilots); clients 16–20 pay full price.
Why year-end MRR can exceed $1,000: per-property pricing scales with portfolio. Demo process — Mahmoud manages, sales partner runs

  • Mahmoud manages all demos — books, qualifies, briefs the partner, follows up
  • The sales partner runs each demo when one is booked — engaged per demo, commission-only
  • Sales partner paid only on close — 50% of one month's subscription (one-time, e.g. $19.50 for a $39/mo client), funded from customer revenue · zero upfront cash from us
  • Pre-call: 2-min Loom + 1-pager sent the day before
  • 30 min: 5 discovery → 15 demo → 5 pricing → 5 commit
  • Proposal sent within 24h, 7-day deadline
  • Follow up at +3d, +7d, +14d

First-customer success

  • White-glove onboarding: 60-min Zoom, listings set up by us
  • Day 7 / 30 / 60 / 90 check-ins on calendar
  • Day 90: case-study interview + referral ask
  • Customer WhatsApp group — community, not a support ticket

Top 3 risks

1 — Calendar sync bugs. Double-bookings lose customers in days. Belal builds sync test suite Week 1; no launch until 50 concurrent bookings pass cleanly.

2 — Slipping October launch. Belal solo on the build (with Claude Code). Cut scope aggressively before missing the date — V1 ships even if a feature is dropped.

3 — Inbound channels don't deliver. All 6 channels are free inbound — no paid ads to fall back on. If they don't produce 10 clients by end of October, we have 2 months to course-correct. Weekly channel review starting Month 1.
